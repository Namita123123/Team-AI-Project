# User Interview Research Questions
**Project:** Multi-Perspective News Aggregator  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-14

---

## Purpose

These questions are designed to understand how people consume news, whether perspective bias is a felt pain point, and what would make them trust and use an AI-powered multi-perspective news tool.

---

## Warm-up

1. How do you typically stay informed about current events? Walk me through your daily routine.
2. Which sources do you rely on most — and why those specifically?

---

## Core Pain Point

3. Have you ever read about the same story from two different sources and noticed they told it very differently? Can you walk me through that experience?
4. Do you ever feel like you're only getting one side of a story? What do you do about it?
5. How much time do you currently spend cross-referencing news sources in a week?

---

## Behaviour & Workarounds

6. When you want a balanced view on a topic, what do you do today? How well does that work?
7. Have you ever used a news aggregator or tool like Google News, Feedly, or AllSides? What did you like or dislike about it?

---

## Solution Validation

8. If a tool could show you three different perspectives on the same story — left, centre, right — would you use it? What would make you trust it?
9. How important is it for you to see *where* each perspective is coming from (source, country, publication)?
10. Would you want the tool to challenge your existing views, or just inform you?
