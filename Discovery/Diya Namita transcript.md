# Topic 1: Slack Channel Summaries and Actionable Insights

### Problem Statement
Employees are members of numerous Slack channels and often miss important discussions because of information overload.

### Proposed Solution
- Summarize discussions across Slack channels.
- Create a personalized daily digest.
- Highlight important updates and action items.
- Prioritize information based on user role and interests.

### Discussion Outcome
Strong candidate due to everyday relevance and a clear pain point.

# Topic 2: AI-Based Carbon Icon Discovery Tool

### Problem Statement
Designers struggle to find appropriate Carbon Design System icons because icon names are often technical or unintuitive.

### Proposed Solution
- Accept screenshots or visual references.
- Recommend matching Carbon icons.
- Provide alternatives and confidence scores.

### Discussion Outcome
Useful but considered a lower-priority problem because it is encountered less frequently.

# Topic 3: Personalized Running Route Recommendation

### Problem Statement

- Personal preferences

### Discussion Outcome

# Topic 4: News Aggregator Showing Multiple Perspectives

### Problem Statement
News coverage varies significantly across regions and publications, leading to biased or incomplete understanding.

### Proposed Solution

- Analyze differing viewpoints.

### Discussion Outcome
Strong candidate because it addresses an important information-consumption challenge.
